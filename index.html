gigigm is a gallery that collects the daily GM (good morning) and GN (good night) image posts by dergigi on the Nostr protocol.

Most days, gigi shares a photograph to greet the morning and close the night. This page pulls those images live from Nostr relays so you can browse the full collection — and scroll back through time.
